# Code of Conduct

> 繁體中文：[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

VoxAvatar aims to keep issues, pull requests, and other project interactions welcoming, professional, and collaborative.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas rather than people; provide reproducible evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Accept good-faith corrections and disclose limitations or uncertainty clearly.
- Protect personal data, credentials, private conversations, and undisclosed vulnerabilities.
- Respect licenses and attribution for characters, models, motions, and other creative work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sustained provocation.
- Publishing another person's personal data, credentials, private messages, or unpatched vulnerabilities.
- Submitting or requesting infringing, unauthorized, or untraceable VRM/VRMA media.
- Impersonation, falsifying verification results, or intentionally disrupting project services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove violating content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation. Report sensitive incidents through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/SanHsien/voxavatar/security/advisories/new). Contact appropriate local services for urgent threats to personal safety.

This policy applies in all VoxAvatar project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
